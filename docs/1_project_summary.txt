Project: Internship Match
Course:  Artificial Intelligence, 4th year, ESIN - UIR
Pair:    Jaafar Trabelsi, Mohammed El Habib Sefiani

PROBLEM
Students waste hours reading internship offers to figure out
whether they actually qualify, and often find out too late that
a requirement was missing.

WHAT WE BUILD
A small system. Input: a student's CV and the text of an
internship offer. Output: a verdict (qualifies / almost /
not suited) and the list of what is missing.
If the offer is too vague, the system refuses instead of guessing.

HOW (4 chained steps)
  1. Extract  - Gemini (LLM)         offer text -> requirements
  2. Match    - embeddings (no LLM)  CV skills + requirements -> scores
  3. Decide   - simple rule (no LLM) scores -> verdict, or refuse
  4. Explain  - Gemini (LLM)         verdict + missing -> short answer

We USE Gemini through its API. We do not build a language model.

THE CLAIM
For students looking for internships, our system gives the correct
verdict on at least 7 out of 10 CV-offer pairs, names the missing
requirements, and refuses when the offer gives too little information.

HOW WE PROVE IT
  - Evaluation set: 15 to 24 CV-offer pairs, correct verdicts
    written BEFORE building the system, including cases to refuse
  - Baseline: Gemini alone on the same cases
  - Ablation: the full system without the Decide step

DATA
  - Public IT internship offers, copied by hand
  - Synthetic CVs written by us (no real personal data)
